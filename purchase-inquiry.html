For organizations that want to own

Apply to purchase FlexTram equipment.

FlexTram sells equipment to approved organizations. Every purchase goes through a short review first: the vehicles carry our name on every panel, and we stay involved after delivery with operator training, route planning, and service. Tell us about your organization and how the trams will run, and Joseph will respond within one business day. Coverage spans the United States and Canada. Renting or looking for a turnkey plan instead? No approval needed; get in touch or request a bid. Buying before December 31? Read the 2026 tax window first.

How approval works.

  1. Submit the form below. Five minutes. Tell us who you are, where the trams will run, and when you need them in service.
  2. Review within one business day. Joseph looks at fit: the venue, the operating plan, who will drive and maintain the equipment, and service coverage. He may ask for a short call.
  3. Approval and terms. Approved organizations receive a quote and a payment structure that matches their calendar: season-start first payment, revenue-timed installments, financing, prepayment pricing, or rental credit toward the purchase.
  4. Delivery, training, route plan. Equipment is delivered ready to run, operators are trained, and the first route plan is built with you.

Submitting an inquiry is not a commitment to buy, and approval is not automatic. We keep the fleet in the hands of operators who will run it well.
