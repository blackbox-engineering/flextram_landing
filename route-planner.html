      This is a planning estimate, not a quote. Fleet size uses Little's Law on each line: cycle time (route length at planning speed plus dwell at every stop) divided by headway (twice your target wait), then checked against peak riders per hour at your seat count. Routes follow mapped public roads where the map data has them (routing by OSRM / OpenStreetMap); parking-lot aisles and service roads are often unmapped, so a drawn route may skirt a lot your tram would cut through. Add a stop along the real path to correct it. Click a line to add shaping points and trace the real tram path; a traced leg uses its drawn length with no road factor. A leg whose road route runs more than twice the straight distance is treated as an unmapped tram path and drawn direct (dashed) instead; tick "direct" on a line to do that for the whole line. Where routing is unavailable the line is a dashed straight-line estimate scaled by the road factor. Every FlexTram is ADA-compliant and seats up to 27 with one operator.
