Fleet size uses Little's Law on each line: cycle time (route length at planning speed plus dwell at every stop) divided by headway (twice your target wait), then checked against peak riders per hour at your seat count. Lines are drawn as straight segments between stops and scaled by the road factor; a site walk replaces that with the real route. Every FlexTram is ADA-compliant and seats up to 27 with one operator.
